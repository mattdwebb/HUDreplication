\section{Purpose}
This note documents evidence that the \texttt{HCITY.x} field in the HUD analysis files is not ad-side city, and instead aligns with recommended-property city values. It also documents the ad-side city recovery procedure from \texttt{adsprocessed\_JPE.rds}.

\section{Data and Matching Design}
The diagnostics use:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{Data/Original/HUDprocessed\_JPE\_census\_042021.rds}
\item \texttt{Data/Original/HUDprocessed\_JPE\_names\_042021.rds}
\item \texttt{Data/Original/HUDprocessed\_JPE\_testscores\_042021.rds}
\item \texttt{Data/Original/adsprocessed\_JPE.rds}
\item \texttt{Data/Original/recsprocessed\_JPE.rds}
\end{itemize}

Ad-side city (\texttt{HCITY\_Ad}) is recovered by joining HUD rows to ads rows on:
\[
\{\texttt{CONTROL, TESTERID, logAdPrice, stfid\_Ad, w2012pc\_Ad, b2012pc\_Ad, a2012pc\_Ad, hisp2012pc\_Ad, oth2012pc\_Ad}\}.
\]

Rec-side cross-check values from \texttt{recsprocessed} are joined on:
\[
\{\texttt{CONTROL, TESTERID, logRecPrice, w2012pc\_Rec, b2012pc\_Rec, a2012pc\_Rec, hisp2012pc\_Rec, oth2012pc\_Rec}\}.
\]

\section{Main Findings}
\input{Output/hcity_diagnostics_main.tex}

The first table shows:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{HCITY.x} equals \texttt{HCITY\_Rec} on all comparable rows.
\item \texttt{HCITY\_Rec} differs from recovered \texttt{HCITY\_Ad} on a large share of comparable rows.
\end{itemize}

\input{Output/hcity_within_control.tex}

Within-control variation in \texttt{HCITY.x} is substantial. Because each trial (\texttt{CONTROL}) corresponds to one advertised property, this variation is inconsistent with \texttt{HCITY.x} being ad-side city.

\input{Output/hcity_recs_crosscheck.tex}

The recsprocessed cross-check confirms that HUD \texttt{HCITY.x} matches rec-side city values.

\section{Interpretation}
Taken together, these diagnostics indicate a source-labeling failure: HUD \texttt{HCITY.x} is functioning as a rec-side city field, not an ad-side city field.

The replication pipeline now includes a separate \texttt{HCITY\_Ad} field merged from ads data, enabling analysis with either city definition:
\begin{itemize}
\item ad-side city fixed effects (\texttt{HCITY\_Ad})
\item rec-side city fixed effects (\texttt{HCITY.x}/\texttt{HCITY\_Rec})
\end{itemize}
